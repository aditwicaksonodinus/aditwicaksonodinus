### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Senior Fullstack Engineer**, **AI/ML Researcher**, and **Academic Lecturer** specializing in modern web architecture, adaptive learning systems (EdTech), and Explainable AI (XAI).

* 🚀 **Building**: Scalable monorepos with Next.js 16 (React 19), Fastify, Neon PostgreSQL (Drizzle ORM), & Flutter.
* 🔬 **Researching**: Q-POMDP Reinforcement Learning algorithms for personalized learning pathways & XAI for legal tech analytics.
* 🎓 **Lecturing**: Bridging foundational CS theory with real-world AI orchestration and secure systems design.
* ⚡ **Performance Obsessed**: Zero-slop code, 100% Lighthouse benchmarks, and Rust-powered token optimization (`RTK`).
